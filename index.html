What it does

Five things, all on your iPhone.

01

Search what's in it

The words printed on the meme, what the picture shows, what's said in a video, and your own tags. Type one word and it's there.

Typed "monday"found all 14 of them
02

Send it to their Meme Bank

Memes you send go into the other person's Inbox in the app, not their Messages or camera roll. Their phone stays clean.

My phone when you sendanother 30 memes
03

Seen, watched, reacted

See when they've seen it or watched it, their thumbs up or down, and whatever they type back. Their words become one-tap buttons.

When she reacts"dead 💀"
04

Work mode

Completely silent. Videos play muted with captions and never stop the music on your speaker. It switches on by itself when something else is playing.

Meme video at workfull volume
05

Make your own

Top and bottom text, caption on top, 2 or 4 panels and stickers, from any photo. Stuck for a line? It suggests one.

Does it upload my photos?

No. Your bank stays on your phone. A meme only leaves it when you send it to someone or make a link, and the server copy is deleted once they've collected it.

TikTok and Instagram?

Saved as the original link with its title and preview. Videos already in your Photos are fully searchable.

Which iPhones?

Anything on iOS 18 or later.
