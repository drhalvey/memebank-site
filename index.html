What it does

Built for the "I saved it somewhere" problem

🔎

Search what's in it

Reads the words printed on every meme, what the picture shows, and what's said in videos. All on your phone.

📬

Send it to their Meme Bank

Memes land in their Inbox, not their Messages or camera roll. No more clogged phones.

💀

Seen, watched, reacted

See when they've seen it or watched it, plus their 👍, 👎 or their own words, like "dead".

✏️

Make your own

Top and bottom text, captions, 2 or 4 panels and stickers, from any photo. Stuck? Tap Suggest a caption.

🔇

Work mode

Totally silent. Videos play muted with captions and never stop the music on your speaker.

🔗

Share with anyone

No app? A Meme Bank link opens in any browser, with 👍 or 👎. TikToks go out as the original link.

Private beta

Want in?

Meme Bank is in a small private beta on iPhone through Apple's TestFlight. To ask for a place, email eh@metroanaes.com.au with the email address you use for your Apple Account.

Ask for a place
Does it upload my photos?

No. Your bank lives on your phone. A meme only leaves it when you send it to someone or make a share link, and the server copy is deleted once they've collected it (share links expire after 30 days unless you keep them).

Can it download TikTok or Instagram videos?

No. Those are saved as the original link with its title and preview picture. Videos you've already saved to Photos are fully searchable.

Will it interrupt my music?

Not in work mode. It switches on by itself whenever something else is playing: videos play silently with captions.

Which iPhones?

iPhone with iOS 18 or later. Suggest a caption uses Apple's on-device AI on newer iPhones and Google's AI otherwise.
