Meme Bank finds any meme you've saved by the words in it.

Type "cat" and you get the cat one. It's an iPhone app. It reads the text on your memes and screenshots, works out what's in the picture, and even listens to what's said in videos. All on your phone.

Get the beta  free, iPhone only, invite via TestFlight

Search what's in it. The words printed on the meme, what the picture shows (dog, goat, gym), what's said in a video, your own tags. No folders needed, though you can have them.

Send them to their Meme Bank, not their phone. Memes you send go into the other person's Inbox in the app. Nothing lands in their Messages or camera roll.

See that they saw it. You get Seen, Watched for videos, a thumbs up or down, and whatever they type back. "Dead", "so random", whatever you two say. Their words become one-tap buttons.

Work mode. The app stays silent: videos play muted with captions and never stop the music on your speaker. It switches on by itself if something else is already playing.

Make your own. Top and bottom text, caption on top, 2 or 4 panels, stickers. Start from any photo or one you've already saved. Stuck for a line? It'll suggest a caption.

And the rest: save from TikTok, Instagram, Photos or any app's Share button; a Show list for the ones you want to show someone later; links anyone can open without the app; optional camera-roll scan if you ask for it.

Questions

Does it upload my photos?
No. Your bank stays on your phone. A meme only leaves it when you send it to someone or make a link, and the server copy is deleted once they've collected it.
Can it download TikTok or Instagram videos?
No. They're saved as the original link with its title and preview. Videos already in your Photos are fully searchable.
Which iPhones?
Anything on iOS 18 or later.
